Terms of Service

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Service purpose

LoL Inhouse Match Manager is a private, non-commercial tool for organizing 5v5 League of Legends matches within a closed community of more than 40 players.

2. Eligibility and access

Access is limited to invited community participants and organizers. Participants are responsible for providing an accurate Riot ID and for following the rules of the community and Riot Games.

3. Service functionality

The service may retrieve current ranked information through official Riot Games APIs. If Tournament API access is approved, it may also create Tournament Code games and record their results automatically. Features may change or be unavailable while the project is being developed.

4. Acceptable use

Participants must not misuse the service, interfere with its operation, attempt unauthorized access, or use it in violation of applicable law or Riot Games terms and policies.

5. No commercial or ranking service

The service has no paid features, advertising, or other monetization. It does not calculate or publish a custom MMR or ELO rating and does not provide public matchmaking.

6. Availability and records

The service is provided on an as-available basis. Organizers may correct or remove inaccurate community records. No guarantee is made that every API response, match record, or feature will always be available or error-free.

7. Privacy

Use of participant information is described in the Privacy Policy.

This project is not endorsed by Riot Games and does not represent an official Riot Games product.
