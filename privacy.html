Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how LoL Inhouse Match Manager processes information for a private League of Legends in-house community. The project is non-commercial and is not intended as a public matchmaking service.

2. Information we process

We may process Riot IDs supplied by community participants and League of Legends ranked or match data returned by official Riot Games APIs. When Tournament API access is approved, Tournament Codes and associated match results may also be processed.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to identify participants, organize private 5v5 matches, display current ranked information, and maintain community match records. The project does not create a custom MMR or ELO rating.

4. Sharing and monetization

Information is not sold, used for advertising, or shared for commercial purposes. Access is limited to what is necessary for the community and its organizers. Data may be processed by service providers used to operate the project and by Riot Games through its official API services.

5. Data collection methods

The project uses participant-provided information and official Riot Games APIs. It does not use external crawling or scraping to collect player information.

6. Retention and requests

Information is kept only while it is reasonably needed to operate the private community and maintain relevant match records. Participants may contact the community organizer to request correction or removal of their stored information.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the project develops or its API access changes. The latest version will always be published on this page.

This project is not endorsed by Riot Games and does not represent an official Riot Games product.
